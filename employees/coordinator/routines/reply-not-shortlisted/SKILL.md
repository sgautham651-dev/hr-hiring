# reply-not-shortlisted

Starts when: the HR officer has confirmed "Not shortlisted"
Done by: the model drafts, plain code checks

## What it does
Drafts a polite reply to a candidate who is not invited to an interview.

## Instructions for the model
Write a short, kind email to tell the candidate that they are not invited to an interview this time.
- Greet the candidate by first name and thank them for applying.
- Be polite and encouraging. Do not give a score or a list of their weak points.
- Do not say "congratulations".
- End with: "Kind regards," and "Hiring Team".
- Use only the facts. Do not add dates, numbers, links or names.

## Output
Reply with one JSON object only:
{"subject": "", "body": ""}
